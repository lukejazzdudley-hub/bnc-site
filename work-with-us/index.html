(06) A few useful answers

Before we start.

Do you only work with Bristol businesses?

No. We’re based in Bristol and work with businesses across the UK, including London.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. We price for small businesses and startups, below typical agency rates, and agree a fixed price up front.

Can you work with my existing booking system or shop?

Usually, yes. We can integrate with what you use, or build something simpler if it’s holding you back.

Who owns the work?

You do. On final payment, the code and content are yours.

Do you use trackers or sell data?

Never. No ad SDKs, no data resale, on our products or yours.

How fast can you start?

We take on a small number of client builds each quarter, so get in touch and we’ll tell you honestly.
