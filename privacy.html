Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what Cadence and Brand Name Changes Ltd (the data controller) collect, why we use it, and the choices and rights available to you. It covers the Cadence mobile app and this website.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, username, authentication identifiers and account ID when you create or sign in to an account.
  • Synced workspace data: song titles, lyrics, formatting, BPM, folder and project organisation, collaborator records, and cover images that you choose to sync.
  • Purchase data: your product, entitlement and subscription status, plus store transaction identifiers. Apple and Google process payment details; we do not receive your full card number.
  • Optional product analytics: a fixed list of feature-use, onboarding and purchase events. Signed-in events use your Cadence account ID; local-only events use a random app-install identifier.
  • Optional crash diagnostics: crash traces, relevant app state, app version, device and operating-system information, and random Firebase/Crashlytics installation identifiers.

Cadence product feedback

When you send product feedback, we collect the answers and contact details you provide, basic submission information, and any optional screenshots or screen recordings you choose to attach. We use this material to research and improve Cadence and, if you opt in, to ask a follow-up question. Feedback and evidence are stored privately with Supabase. We do not use that evidence publicly or in advertising unless we ask you separately and you agree.

We keep research responses while they remain useful to product development. You can ask to access or delete yours by emailing hello@brandnamechanges.com.

Project enquiries

When you send a project enquiry, we collect your name, email address, business name, optional website, selected services and message. Brand Name Changes Ltd uses these details only to respond, prepare a proposal and discuss the work you requested. We do not add you to a mailing list, sell these details or share them for another organisation’s marketing.

Our Supabase enquiry endpoint validates the submission and uses Resend to email it to our studio’s Google-hosted inbox. These providers process the data on our behalf; the enquiry endpoint does not store a copy of your message in its database. An hourly salted network-address hash and attempt count help prevent spam, with old counters removed on subsequent requests after two hours. Provider security logs are separate. We delete enquiries that do not lead to work within 12 months of the last contact; project and accounting records follow the retention requirements of that work. Email brandnamechanges@gmail.com to request access or deletion. This form adds no trackers or third-party analytics.

Your lyrics and audio

Cadence does not send lyrics, titles, transcripts, audio, filenames, searches or free-form error messages as product analytics. Mic takes remain on the device where they were recorded. Audio leaves your device only when you deliberately export, share or upload it through a collaboration feature; private shared audio is stored in access-controlled storage for the people you share the song with.

Cadence accesses the microphone only for a recording you start. It does not access your contacts or location, and it accesses photos or files only when you choose an item to import or use as cover art.

Analytics and crash-reporting choice

Help improve Cadence is optional and stays off until you choose to enable it in Settings › Privacy, Data & Help. The preference controls both product analytics and Crashlytics diagnostics. Turning it off stops new collection, clears pending on-device analytics counters and resets local Firebase Analytics state. Turning it back on records new events only.

We do not set a Crashlytics user ID or deliberately attach your creative content to crash reports. Crashlytics uses random installation identifiers to group affected app installations and retains crash reports and their associated identifiers according to Firebase's service retention policy.

How we use data

We use data to provide and secure Cadence, sync your workspace, manage accounts and entitlements, process collaboration, diagnose reliability problems, understand which product flows work, communicate about your account, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data.

Advertising and attribution

The current Cadence app does not include cross-company advertising tracking and does not use Apple's advertising identifier. Product and purchase analytics are used to measure Cadence itself. If we later add cross-app or cross-website tracking, we will update this policy and the store disclosures and request any permission required before collection.

Service providers and international processing

Supabase provides authentication, database, private file storage and product counters; Firebase / Google Analytics provides product analytics and account-identifier deletion processing; Firebase Crashlytics provides crash diagnostics; RevenueCat manages subscriptions and entitlements; Apple and Google provide sign-in and store purchases; and Resend provides transactional email. These providers may process data in the UK, EEA, United States and other locations where they operate, using contractual and technical safeguards.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and synced workspace data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account from Settings › Profile › Delete account or use our account deletion page. Account deletion removes your first-party workspace and account-linked product events and queues deletion of the same account identifier from Google Analytics. We do not set your Cadence account ID in Crashlytics; its installation-scoped diagnostics expire under Firebase's retention policy. Store transaction records may be retained where required for tax, accounting, fraud prevention or legal compliance.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access to, correction of, portability of, restriction of, objection to, or deletion of your personal data. You may also withdraw a consent where consent is the basis for processing. Email support@brandnamechanges.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

Children

Cadence is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum digital-consent age in their country, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when Cadence or its service providers change. Material changes will appear here with a new date. Questions or requests: support@brandnamechanges.com.

Optional website measurement

The Cadence website offers optional counts of page views and outbound clicks to the App Store and Google Play. Nothing is collected by this feature until you allow it. You can decline or change your choice in Privacy choices at the bottom of a Cadence page. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track disable these counts.

When allowed, the payload contains only a Cadence page path, a broad source group (such as Google, Bing, referral, direct or campaign), the event type and the destination store. It does not contain your lyric, rhyme query, email, account ID, full referrer or URL query string. A preference is saved locally, and coarse entry source/path are kept in tab session storage. No analytics visitor identifier is created. Store clicks are attributed to the first page where measurement was allowed; counts are not unique people, installs or purchases.

Counts are processed through our existing Supabase infrastructure. Like any request, network infrastructure receives an IP address; our analytics tables do not store the raw address. A domain-separated hourly hash is used only for abuse limiting. Subsequent events remove hourly buckets older than a two-hour cutoff and daily aggregate rows older than a 180-day cutoff; cleanup is event-driven, not a guaranteed fixed expiry deadline. Provider operational/security logs are separate from these aggregate tables.

Declining leaves the website and app-store links fully usable. The app’s own analytics preference is separate. Search Console query reports are aggregated reports from Google, not joined to individual website visitors.
