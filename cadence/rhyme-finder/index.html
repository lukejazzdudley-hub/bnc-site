    What counts as a multisyllabic rhyme?

    A multisyllabic rhyme matches sounds across two or more syllables. In this tool, “motion” and “ocean” match from the stressed vowel through the ending. “Light” and “night” are perfect rhymes with a one-syllable match, so they appear in Perfect rhymes.

    A long word does not automatically make a multisyllabic rhyme. The length of the matching sound is what matters.

    How to use the results

    Read the candidates out loud in your accent. Put the word back into your line and check both its meaning and rhythm. Results are grouped by word syllable count and alphabetically, not rated for artistic quality.

    This free tool uses American English dictionary pronunciations. It can include uncommon words and proper names, and it does not cover every accent, slang word or phrase. It finds exact sound tails, not slant rhymes. The full Cadence app offers a broader writing workspace and additional rhyme modes.

    Private searches, no sign-in

    The pronunciation dictionary loads on your first search. Matching happens in your browser; the tool does not send your word to a server or save it. The website host still receives ordinary requests for page and dictionary files.
