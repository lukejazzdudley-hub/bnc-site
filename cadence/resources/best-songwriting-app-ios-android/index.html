Keep the beat, the words and the take together

Download Cadence for iPhone and iPad or get Cadence for Android. It’s built for artists who already have a sound in mind and want to hear their words become a performance.

Load your instrumental, choose the passage you want to loop and revise the lyric while it plays. If you’re stuck on an ending, use on-device suggestions for perfect, slant, multisyllabic, multi-word or assonant rhymes. Turn on the syllable counter if you want to check the shape of the line.

Record two deliveries and keep the stronger take with the song. With Pro, assign takes to timed sections, build the arrangement in the mobile DAW and share a mix or aligned WAV stems. The difference is the handoff from writing to recording: the take you made while finding the flow can stay useful when it’s time to arrange the demo.

Start with three free notes. Check the current offer in your store before buying Pro.

A real decision you can make before arranging

Imagine a hook that reads well but pushes the last word past the downbeat. Loop the last two bars of the verse and first two of the hook. Record one version with the original line and another that drops two syllables. Keep both takes beside the lyric. If the shorter line gives the hook room to breathe, use that take in the draft and leave the first one available as a reference.

Try the same exercise with the browser syllable counter before opening the app. Then use the section recording walkthrough to see where the take belongs.

Put one of your own songs through the comparison

  1. Bring one instrumental you have permission to use and four lines you’ve already written. Use the same material and device wherever you’re comparing a shared platform.
  2. Loop one section. Try an alternative rhyme and record a second delivery with the same timing. Close the song, reopen it and find both takes.
  3. Choose the delivery you want to keep. If finishing the arrangement on your phone matters, move the verse and hook into a timeline and test the output you’d actually send.
