Original writing exercise

Make one take before you revise.

Record the same short hook twice: once spoken naturally and once over your beat. Mark any word whose pronunciation or syllable count changes. Build a second line around the performed ending and compare the pair by ear. If you switch languages, make that switch explicit in your notes rather than asking the English finder to infer it.

Keep the first take, revise the line and record a second version. Compare the two in the context of the song. You can keep writing in Cadence’s connected songwriting workflow.

Before you start

Where can I download Cadence in South Africa?

Use the South Africa App Store listing for iPhone and iPad, or the Google Play link on this page for Android. The store checks compatibility and availability for your account and device.

How do I check the local Cadence Pro price?

South African storefront accounts can check available purchases in rand (ZAR). Use the linked listing for the current offer; a conversion from an overseas article is not a store price. Your account and device determine the options shown by Apple or Google.

Does signing in back up all my recordings?

No. Account sync is not a recording backup. Audio stays on your device unless you share it; keep exports of important recordings and finished work.

Does the free web rhyme finder support every language and accent?

No. The free browser finder currently uses American English pronunciations and a limited original phrase bank. Check results aloud in your own accent. The website tool and the Cadence app use different rhyme engines.
