Separate English rhyme support from multilingual writing

A verse may move between English and another language, or use a romanised spelling that an English dictionary cannot recognise. The free website rhyme finder supports English only today. This regional page does not add Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil or other language dictionaries, and the Indian Apple listing currently declares an English app interface.

Keep the line as you intend to perform it and record a reference take. Check any transcription against that audio, especially where languages change or a word has been romanised. Search an English ending when that is useful, then return to the whole verse to judge the sound and meaning together.

Try the free English rhyme finder or move a lyric draft into a recorded demo.

Original writing exercise

Make one take before you revise.

Write a four-line verse with an intentional language switch if that is part of your writing. Mark the exact switch and record the verse before editing its spelling. Compare where your breath and stress fall on each side. Keep those performance notes beside the lyric instead of forcing both passages into English rhyme results.

Keep the first take, revise the line and record a second version. Compare the two in the context of the song. You can keep writing in Cadence’s connected songwriting workflow.

Before you start

Where can I download Cadence in India?

Use the India App Store listing for iPhone and iPad, or the Google Play link on this page for Android. The store checks compatibility and availability for your account and device.

How do I check the local Cadence Pro price?

The Indian storefront links let eligible accounts check available purchases in Indian rupees (INR). The page does not promise a payment method or a plan that your store has not offered. Confirm your device compatibility and the final purchase terms on Apple or Google.

Does signing in back up all my recordings?

No. Account sync is not a recording backup. Audio stays on your device unless you share it; keep exports of important recordings and finished work.

Does the free web rhyme finder support every language and accent?

No. The free browser finder currently uses American English pronunciations and a limited original phrase bank. Check results aloud in your own accent. The website tool and the Cadence app use different rhyme engines.
