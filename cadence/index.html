Built around the song

Less app-switching. More useful feedback.

Start with what already exists

Open a blank lyric, record an idea, scan a handwritten page, or share text, audio files and Voice Memos into the same song workspace.

Hear the difference between rhymes

Explore Perfect, Slant, Multi-syllable, Multi-word and Assonance results from an on-device pronunciation engine.

Write inside the pocket

Load a beat, see its real waveform, scrub precisely and drag independent A/B points around the section you are writing.

Make the draft audible

Pull selected lyrics into a read-only Draft Demo, record alternatives and preserve each section's timing before final production.

Finish without starting over

Continue the timed draft in a multitrack DAW, reorder lanes, trim clips, loop, zoom and record final takes against the same timeline.

Make decisions with your ears

Apply vocal FX, compare Dry and Wet at the same play point, adjust the live mixer, then export a demo or aligned stems.
