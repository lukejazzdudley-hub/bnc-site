The connected workflow

Capture the spark.
Keep the momentum.

Four real product moments. One continuous song.

01 / Capture

Start with what you already have.

Open a new lyric, share a Voice Memo or audio file into Cadence, scan a handwritten page, or record the thought before it disappears.

  • Import text and audio
  • Scan handwritten lyrics
  • Transcribe a take on-device

02 / Write

Find the rhyme that earns its place.

Search beyond obvious endings. Cadence separates Perfect, Slant, Multi-syllable, Multi-word and Assonance results—and keeps them beside the lyric.

The rhyme engine runs on-device, so writing stays quick and private.

03 / Shape

Give the words somewhere to land.

Load the real beat waveform, set independent A/B points and map selected lyrics into timed song sections. Build a Draft Demo before moving the song into the DAW.

04 / Finish

Make the decisions with your ears.

Record final takes in the same multitrack DAW. Compare Dry and Wet at one play point, shape vocal FX, balance the live mixer, then export a demo or aligned stems.

  • Multitrack DAW
  • Vocal FX
  • Demo & stems export
