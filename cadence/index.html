The connected workflow

Capture the spark.
Keep the momentum.

Four real product moments. One continuous song.

01 / Capture

Start with what you already have.

Open a new lyric, share a Voice Memo or audio file into Cadence, scan a handwritten page, or record the thought before it disappears.

  • Import text and audio
  • Scan handwritten lyrics
  • Transcribe a take on-device

02 / Write

Find the rhyme that earns its place.

Search beyond obvious endings. Cadence separates Perfect, Slant, Multi-syllable, Multi-word and Assonance results—and keeps them beside the lyric.

The rhyme engine runs on-device, so writing stays quick and private.

03 / Shape

Give the words somewhere to land.

Load the real beat waveform, set independent A/B points and map selected lyrics into timed song sections. Build a Draft Demo before moving the song into the DAW.

04 / Finish

Make the decisions with your ears.

Record final takes in the same multitrack DAW. Compare Dry and Wet at one play point, shape vocal FX, balance the live mixer, then export a demo or aligned stems.

  • Multitrack DAW
  • Vocal FX
  • Demo & stems export

What is Flow by Cadence?

Flow by Cadence is another way to find Cadence, the songwriting app from Brand Name Changes. The app is listed as Cadence: Lyrics & Rhymes on the App Store and as Cadence: Rap Lyrics & Rhymes on Google Play.

Can I start with a beat or a freestyle?

Yes. Start with a beat, a written lyric or a recorded idea. Keep the words, beat and vocal takes with the song, then develop the arrangement and demo in the same workspace.

Is Cadence available for iPhone, iPad and Android?

Cadence is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android. Use the regional download guide to open a local storefront and check device compatibility.

Is Cadence free to try?

Start with three free notes. Cadence Pro unlocks unlimited new work and the complete Draft-to-DAW workflow, including vocal FX and exports. Your store shows the available plans, local currency and current price before purchase.

Does signing in back up all my recordings?

No. Account sync is not a recording backup. Audio stays on your device unless you share it; keep exports of important recordings and finished work.

Does the free web rhyme finder support every language and accent?

No. The free browser finder currently uses American English pronunciations and a limited original phrase bank. Check results aloud in your own accent. The website tool and the Cadence app use different rhyme engines.
