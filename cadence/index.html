From spark to demo

The working parts of a song, connected.

Cadence follows the actual creative sequence: catch an idea, develop it, hear it against the beat and return later with the context intact.

✦

Rhymes in the line

See perfect, slant and internal rhyme families as you type. Tap a word for focused suggestions. Downloaded language packs keep the core writing tools available offline.

≡

One song workspace

Keep lyrics, sections, notes, BPM, beat and voice memos attached to the same song instead of rebuilding context every time you switch apps.

●

Capture the take

Record melody ideas and A/B two takes inside the song. Your recordings remain local to the device where you made them.

↥

Bring old ideas back

Import existing lyrics, scan a handwritten page, and use on-device transcription to turn a voice memo into editable words without sending the audio to the cloud.

↻

Loop, arrange, bounce

Loop the part of the beat you are writing over. In Draft Demo, arrange memos against the beat on a timeline and bounce a rough demo to share.

◐

Make the room yours

Choose from 27 theme families, with light and dark variants plus separate contrast controls. Your workspace can change without changing the song.
