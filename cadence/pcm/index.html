Nigerian Pidgin pack and how you dey talk

Download the Naijá pack when internet dey. After that, the app rhyme engine dey work for your device. Use the spelling and pronunciation wey you go perform. If you mix Pidgin with English or Yorùbá, test the whole line; the language packs no be the same thing.

From type beat reach your first recording

Import audio wey you get permission to use. Set A and B so one short part go repeat while you write beside the player. Record your voice idea, compare versions, then arrange your verse and hook for sections. Na you dey write and perform the song.

Feature guide (English) · Recording (English)

Free start and wetin Pro cover

You fit start with three free notes. The full Draft-to-DAW work, including arrangement and export, need Pro; check the plans for your store. Rhyme pack no mean say every app screen dey Pidgin, and e no guarantee correct transcription. Export important recordings before you change device. Account sync no be backup of all your takes.

Questions wey people dey ask

I fit write and record on top beat?

Yes. Import audio wey you get permission to use, repeat one part with A–B, and keep your recording with the lyrics.
