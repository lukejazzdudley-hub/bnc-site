Rhyme is sound, not spelling

Perfect rhymes share their stressed vowel and following sounds. Slant rhymes leave room for a looser connection. Multisyllabic rhymes carry a match across more than one syllable. Each gives you a different choice when the obvious ending feels forced.

Cadence puts those choices beside your writing. Highlighting shows possible connections; your accent, delivery and intended meaning decide which ones belong.

Explore perfect and multisyllabic English rhymes.

Write against the part of the beat that matters

Set an A–B loop around the section you are working on. Keep the compact player beside the fullscreen editor, and use beat markers to return to a point you want to rehearse. You can try a line several ways without searching through the entire instrumental each time.

Check the delivery, not just the line length

A syllable count gives you a reference, but two lines with the same count can land differently. Record a take and listen for the stressed words, breaths and spaces. Move from a written draft into Arrange and the DAW when you are ready to build a demo.
