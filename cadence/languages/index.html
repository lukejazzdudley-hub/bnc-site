A songwriting app for Spanish lyrics

Cadence brings your Spanish lyric, instrumental and vocal takes into the same song on Android, iPhone and iPad. You write the words and perform them; the app helps you explore rhymes, loop the beat and keep the versions together.

  1. Set up Spanish rhyme suggestions. Download the Spanish pack while connected. In Languages and suggestion order, choose where Spanish sits alongside your other languages. The pack supports rhyme exploration; it does not mean every app screen is translated into Spanish.
  2. Write against the instrumental. Import audio you have permission to use and set an A–B loop around a short passage. Keep the lyric beside the player and leave room for breath.
  3. Check the line in your voice. Record a rough take and listen for the stressed words, pauses and meaning. Say suggestions in your own pronunciation before choosing one.
  4. Develop the song. Keep writing in the same project, then use sections and the Pro draft-to-DAW workflow when you are ready to build a demo. Start with three free notes; consult the store for Pro plans.

Prefer to read in Spanish? Cadence: app para escribir canciones y letras sobre beats explains the workflow. To try a lookup without installing the app, open the free Spanish rhyme dictionary. The browser dictionary uses independent public data and has its own pronunciation and coverage limits; it is not a preview of every app result.

App packs are separate from interface language

English is built in. Cadence’s app catalog includes 20 downloadable language packs, including French and Spanish. Download the packs you need while connected. The pronunciation-based rhyme engine runs on device. A rhyme pack does not mean every app screen is translated or every recording can be transcribed accurately.

Check a suggestion aloud in your own pronunciation. Language support does not remove accent, vocabulary or performance differences. Read the current store description and check the app’s language picker before relying on a specific pack.
