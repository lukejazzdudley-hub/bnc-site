Set up हिन्दी rhyme suggestions

  1. Open the app’s language picker and download हिन्दी while connected. English is built in; this is one of 20 downloadable packs.
  2. In the language settings, enable the languages you want and adjust their suggestion order. Put the language you are actively writing first; keep another enabled for a bilingual passage.
  3. Write a short line and open the rhyme inspector on a word. Try the suggestion in the line, record it and compare the sound in context.

A rhyme pack is not a translation of every app screen. It also does not promise accurate transcription for every accent, complete vocabulary coverage or a language-specific poetic meter counter.

A Hindi listening check

Keep a Devanagari version of the line when testing the Hindi pack. Romanised Hindi has variable spellings; compare your pronunciation and do not assume transliterations give the same lookup.

Try a short writing session

Write a Hindi verse in Devanagari about a place you return to. Make a spoken take, then a sung take over two loops. Listen for how words connect and where you need breath. Revise the wording in the draft instead of relying on a Romanised spelling to explain the sound.

Writing across languages

If you write a Hindi–English hook, preserve a Devanagari version of the Hindi words. Put the active language first when searching suggestions. Compare the same phrase in speech and melody, and check loanwords in your own pronunciation rather than assuming an English reading.

Write to a type beat, then build the sections

Import a licensed instrumental or a beat you made yourself. “Type beat” describes a style; it does not give you permission to release someone else’s recording. Set an A–B loop around a useful passage and write beside the player. A short loop lets you hear where the stressed words land.

Record a rough voice memo before polishing every word. Move between spoken and sung versions, compare pauses and keep the take with the strongest delivery. The app provides writing and recording tools; you author the song.

When the idea is ready, use Draft sections and arrangement to develop the order, then follow the export guide for sharing audio or moving work to your DAW. The detailed manual and its current control labels are in English.

App pack, browser tool and free plan

Start with three free notes. The complete Draft-to-DAW workflow, including arrangement and export, requires Pro. Read your store listing for current plans and availability.

The Hindi pack works in the app; this page does not provide a Hindi web dictionary. The separate English, French and Spanish browser tools have their own data and limits.

Export important recordings before changing devices. Account sync is not a backup of every vocal take. Learn the current feature locations and controls before relying on a release workflow.

Before your next session

Can I write Hindi songs on Android?

Yes. Cadence is available for Android, iPhone and iPad. Download the हिन्दी pack for rhyme suggestions, write your own lyrics and record ideas against imported audio you have permission to use. Check the store for device compatibility.

Is the language pack a translation of the whole app?

No. A rhyme pack is not a translation of every app screen, a guarantee of transcription accuracy or a complete analysis of poetic meter. Listen to suggestions in your own pronunciation.

Is there a free Hindi browser rhyme dictionary here?

This site does not currently offer a Hindi browser rhyme dictionary. The downloadable app pack is a different product from the site's English, French and Spanish web tools.
