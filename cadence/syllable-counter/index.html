    Try the rhythm of these two lines

    The last train left at midnight
    7 syllables
    I heard the drums through the rain
    7 syllables

    Both lines have seven syllables. Try holding “train” in the first and “rain” in the second. Listen to how differently the same count can fit the beat. A lyric line counts as one row here. A musical bar depends on the timing of your performance. Paste just the two lines you want to compare if you are revising a crowded bar. Tap through them and listen for the stressed words before cutting any syllables.

    When the count needs your ear

    The fire fades
    3–4 syllables
    I hear the 808
    3 known syllables

    “Fire” has one- and two-syllable pronunciations in the dictionary. Read “808” the way you would perform it, such as “eight-oh-eight,” and add that count yourself.

    How the count handles pronunciation

    The counter counts vowel sounds in the public English pronunciation data used by our rhyme finder, based on the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. Where the dictionary has more than one syllable count for a word, the result shows a range. Unfamiliar words and numerals are marked separately, so the displayed count is a subtotal. Dialect, contractions and sung delivery can change what you hear.

    Read your syllables, stresses and pauses against an instrumental, or turn on the in-app counter while writing.

    Common questions

    Does this tool upload my lyrics?

    The pronunciation dictionary downloads on the first count. The text is processed in a browser worker and is not uploaded or stored by this tool.

    How many syllables fit in a rap bar?

    It depends on tempo, subdivisions, pickups, rests and delivery. Compare the line counts with a recording over the actual beat.
