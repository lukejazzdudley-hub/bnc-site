Keep the whole song together.

A line in Notes. A melody in Voice Memos. A beat somewhere else. Cadence brings the pieces into one song so you can keep writing.

A songwriting notebook with room to grow

Start with a lyric, an imported recording or a new vocal take. Keep those ideas with the song they belong to. Projects help you group songs into an EP, album or collection without losing the individual drafts.

You do not need to start with a complete production. Capture the next line while it is fresh, then return to the same song to develop it.

Find a rhyme without leaving the page

Rhyme highlighting helps you see patterns across a verse. The inspector lets you explore perfect rhymes, slant rhymes, multisyllabic matches and assonance as you revise. Syllable counts can help you compare lines; saying the words over your beat is still the test of how they flow.

Try the free English rhyme finder before installing.

From a draft to something you can hear

Bring in a beat, loop the part you are writing to, and record takes alongside your words. Use Arrange to sketch the song structure, then open the DAW to position clips, balance tracks and work with effects.

See the Springtime Showers arrangement and DAW walkthrough.

Your account and recordings

Cadence keeps recordings on your device unless you share them. Account sync and local audio are different: signing in on a second device does not automatically transfer every recording. Keep exports of important work.
