# Independent studio concepts with optional motion

The client-services concepts previously shared much of their composition and a common large footer. The electrical plug model was rejected; the wine comparison intentionally exposed a sparse wireframe, which read as a broken page. The domain homepage also failed to lead reliably into the service offer.

Use complete static content and independent concept compositions. Keep the practical services offer and visible enquiry actions on the homepage. Give each concept an interaction appropriate to its customer journey: a wine menu and table, lighting and an estimate, movement and an appointment. Keep fictional-business labels and local-only demo confirmations.

Original raw WebGL fields enhance locally hosted imagery without importing another renderer. The existing pinned Motion runtime handles small entrance effects. The homepage activates its decorative shader only after an interaction, avoiding shader compilation during the critical first paint. Concepts activate near the viewport; capped resolution, visibility observers and reduced-motion/save-data fallbacks bound their cost.

This keeps the current GitHub Pages build and working enquiry backend. It introduces no frontend framework or new service dependency. The tradeoff is ownership of small shader/lifecycle code, covered by real-browser checks of rendered changes, offscreen stopping and complete fallbacks. Technical passing results do not establish creative acceptance; inspect the compositions and motion separately.
