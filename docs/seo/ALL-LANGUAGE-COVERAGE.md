# All-language organic search coverage

## Scope and acceptance

Cover built-in English and all 20 downloadable language packs verified against
the mobile catalog and public production manifest on 16 October 2026. Keep
Brazilian and European Portuguese separate. Catalog support is not a claim of
translated app screens, perfect transcription or native editorial acceptance.

Publish static native-language product introductions and English pack workflow
guides. Preserve existing French/Spanish URLs and browser dictionaries. Each
pack guide must explain its actual setup, a language-specific listening check,
beat-first writing, recording and plan boundaries. No fabricated dictionary
tools, country offices, ratings or keyword-volume figures.

Use self-canonicals, reciprocal alternates for equivalent product introductions,
correct script/direction and crawlable directory links. Pack guides are separate
English articles, not hreflang equivalents. Preserve the Onyx design, existing
optional 3D manual and approved product media; reading requires no JavaScript.

## Research and outcomes

Track English and native broad app intent, Android/iPhone intent, rhyme support,
beat writing and bilingual workflows for every catalog entry. Query seeds are
hypotheses until country-level search data validates demand. Research-provider
results are competitor discovery, not a reproducible Google top-ten audit.
Unknown monthly volume and organic difficulty remain unknown; advertising
competition and long-tail length cannot stand in for organic difficulty.

Search Console query/page/country/device observations are the ranking baseline.
A page-one outcome needs observed positions and clicks for a named query,
country, device and period. Passing tests, publishing pages, accepted sitemap
submissions and indexing alone do not satisfy that outcome. Reassess landing
pages from real queries after discovery rather than producing every permutation
as a separate page.

### Evidence collected on 16 October 2026

- `all-language-intent-map.csv`: 248 mapped query hypotheses across all packs
  and built-in English, covering broad app, platform, rhyme, beat, recording,
  bilingual and adjacent-tool intent. The map names candidate research markets,
  not claimed store availability. Unknown demand/difficulty cells are explicit.
- `all-language-competitor-observations.csv`: Bing's displayed top-result lists
  for 16 native app queries, using All countries/languages/devices and the UI's
  3 M period. These are Bing observations, not a Google top-ten ranking audit.
  Thirteen reports displayed insufficient trend data; Arabic, Korean and
  Indonesian country/trend panels were still incomplete when results arrived.
  No numeric traffic figure is derived from these incomplete panels.
- Bing rate-limited the next Filipino query. Filipino, Vietnamese, Yorùbá,
  Nigerian Pidgin and the new English baseline remain incomplete in this Bing
  batch. Separate web discovery covered these languages; it does not substitute
  for country-specific volume or a reproducible SERP audit.
- Existing historical Keyword Planner bands remain in `intent-traffic-map.csv`.
  Do not apply a global English band to a translated query. Google advertising
  competition is not organic competition.

The broad lists expose substantial intent ambiguity: AI generators occur beside
human writing tools in Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Turkish,
Indonesian, Malay and Polish. German/Italian lists include comparison articles
and store listings. Japanese includes dedicated writing products and memo tools;
Korean includes writing products, native blogs and AI tools. A generic translated
“songwriting app” title alone is insufficient evidence of competitive fit.

The implementation therefore gives direct writing intent a product introduction,
English pack-specific intent a setup/listening/exercise guide, and existing
dictionary intent the actual working English/French/Spanish tool. Searches for
other browser dictionaries or automatic language-specific meter remain partial
fits: no fictional tool or blanket capability claim is added. This separation is
an editorial response to observed result types, not proof of easy competition.

### What must still be measured

For each query/market/device cohort, capture the current Google leading results,
result format, title/intent match and quality of the actual tool/demo. Record
observed demand from country-level exports when available. Prioritise direct-fit
queries with demonstrated impressions and a useful page gap. Keep broad head
terms and specific workflow terms in the same plan, but classify organic
competition only after examining the relevant results and their authority.

Use jointly dimensioned Search Console query + page + country + device data for
attribution; separate exported tabs cannot be joined as if they identify the same
clicks. Compare named time windows after deployment. An average position of ten
or better is an aggregate observation, not proof of every user's page-one result.
No new page-one result is claimed by this release.

## Locale identifiers

Routes preserve `/pt/`, `/pt-pt/` and `/tl/`; HTML/schema use `pt-BR`, `pt-PT`
and `fil`. Tagalog/Filipino copy uses `tl` in Google's ISO 639-1 alternate
annotations. Nigerian Pidgin retains valid `pcm` HTML/schema language and visible
links, but is outside the Google hreflang cluster because Google's documented
format has no ISO 639-1 code for it. It is never relabelled `en-NG`.

Sources: [Google localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions),
[Google international sites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites),
[Bing keyword research](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/keyword-research-628070b6).

## Maintenance

Update catalog metadata from release evidence, not unreleased mobile source.
Keep raw account exports and pack payloads outside the public repository. Native
copy is an editorial draft until independent fluent review; do not label it
human approved. Update the generated measurement route allowlist with sitemap
routes before deploying the event function. Validate all links, locale/schema
contracts, RTL layout, locked responsive widths and no-JavaScript content.
