## Public evidence reviewed

- Cadence Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cadenceapp.mobile
- Song Cage Mobile: https://songcage.com/mobile/
- Loopin: https://onloopin.com/

Current result discovery surfaced app stores, product pages and app shortlists.
Loopin also markets beat/lyrics/takes together, so that phrase alone is not a
unique competitive advantage. Song Cage explicitly describes its phone product
as capture-first and places full arrangement on desktop/web. Cadence documents
Draft, arrangement and DAW on mobile; emphasize the specific path and recorded
evidence, without claiming universal superiority or inventing missing rival
features. No paid keyword database or backlink audit was used.
