# Anonymous artist feedback

The homepage contains three semantic blockquotes in server-delivered HTML under
`#artist-feedback`, with anonymous captions and relevant internal links. No script
or carousel is required to read them. This makes the text available to crawlers;
it does not guarantee indexing, rankings or review-rich results.

Two excerpts come from user-supplied tester survey responses; the third comes
from an incoming message visibly checked in the supplied screen recording.
Each figure represents a distinct tester. Spelling, capitalisation and punctuation
are lightly edited; meaning is preserved. The page discloses selected excerpts,
individual experiences and early testing with complimentary access.

Raw sources, frame captures, transcripts, contact details, social handles,
collaborator identities and source timestamps stay outside this public repository.
No Review or AggregateRating markup, star score, verified-purchase claim, Google
endorsement or universal superiority claim is added. Never quote the founder’s
outgoing messages as customer feedback. Do not turn anticipation of a feature
into a claim that the tester used it.

Maintain the section in cadence/index.html and its styles in cadence/cadence.css.
Run artist_feedback_test.py alongside normal release checks. Check the responsive
layout and internal destinations before publishing any further quote changes.
