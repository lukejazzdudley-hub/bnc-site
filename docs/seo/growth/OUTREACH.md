Search Sent mail before sending duplicates. Link live pages only, describe any demo-build limitations, and offer to answer questions without promising paid review access or a response date. Earned coverage depends on editorial interest; sending an email does not earn a backlink.

Native review is a separate request: send the compact packet in LANGUAGE-REVIEW.md to qualified native practitioners, ask whether they are interested and available, and agree any fees before committing. Source maintainers can assess dataset issues, but their reply is not automatically songwriter/editorial acceptance.
